## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an Apple-inspired 3D Liquid Glass aesthetic tailored for smart personal expense and budget management. It bridges institutional wealth management with tactile, hyper-sensory digital craftsmanship. Engineered specifically for students, young founders, and early-career professionals, it transforms everyday budgeting, manual expense entry, and goal tracking into an engaging, luminous experience.

The design movement fuses **Glassmorphism** with **Tactile / Skeuomorphic** realism. Rather than relying on flat 2D glass approximations or sterile neomorphic mud, surfaces behave like physical, water-clear polished acrylic lenses. They feature prominent convex rim highlights, crisp refraction speculars along top beveled edges, deep ambient refraction shadows, and heavy backdrop frosted diffusion. The interface evokes feelings of financial clarity, optical precision, and modern luxury.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture establishes deep oceanic richness overlaid with liquid luminosity and an organic warm foundation:

- **Primary (`#0A2E44` - Midnight Sapphire):** Anchors high-contrast typography, hero balance cards, primary navigational structure, and deep liquid glass backing cards.
- **Secondary (`#173A33` - Ocean Night):** A deep botanical jewel-tone that grounds secondary status indicators, nested containers, and dark glass reflections.
- **Tertiary (`#99E1D9` - Signature Turquoise):** The luminous light-source tone. Applied to interactive focus rings, active pill indicators, glass rim glows, progress tracks, and primary call-to-action highlights.
- **Neutral (`#F6F1E9` - Soft Ivory):** The warm architectural canvas that prevents stark digital fatigue, providing subtle atmospheric contrast behind frosted translucent lenses.

### Semantic & Surface Colors
- **Income Green (`#15846C`):** Positive cash inflows, achieved monthly savings milestones, and upward balance trends.
- **Expense Coral (`#D96969`):** Daily expense debits, over-budget category warnings, and threshold exceedances.
- **Warning Amber (`#E5A247`):** Upcoming bill commitments, 80% category budget depletion, and neutral cautionary states.
- **Glass Matrix:** Translucent white layers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)` to `0.72`) calibrated with crystalline light refractions to hover weightlessly above both ivory backgrounds and deep sapphire containers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces high legibility and structure across three deliberate type families:

1. **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Expressive, geometric, and welcoming. Applied to dashboard greeting headers, category titles, and high-impact account summaries. Negative tracking tightens larger optical silhouettes, pairing naturally with glass capsules.
2. **Body & Microcopy (Inter):** Neutral, utilitarian, and legible across frosted glass translucent planes. High x-height ensures immediate comprehension in transaction lists, form prompts, and tooltips.
3. **Financial Data & Ledger Figures (JetBrains Mono):** Financial values, cash flows, and Rupee amounts (`₹`) leverage monospaced figures with proportional tabular alignment (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). This guarantees decimal and comma alignment across stacked ledgers, monthly budget breakdown bars, and running totals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements a fluid grid system aligned to an 8px base grid (subdivided into 4px micro-steps for compact badges and glass pill button insets):

- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer screen padding and `1rem` gutters. Glass cards span full screen width; navigation docks as an ultra-tactile floating liquid pill at the screen base.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column fluid layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Metric cards and category budgets arrange into a 2-column tiled array.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters, contained to a maximum width of 1280px for focused single-ledger visibility.

Component spacing retains generous breathing room to accommodate dimensional shadows and specular bevels. Liquid cards utilize `1.5rem` to `2rem` internal padding to prevent interactive content from clipping against translucent beveled borders.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through dimensional liquid optics rather than standard drop shadows. The design simulates light traveling through high-index refractive glass:

### The Liquid Glass Formula
Every elevated glass surface features four coordinated layers:
1. **Backdrop Blur:** `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)` to diffuse underlying content into soft color fields.
2. **Convex Rim Highlight (Top Inset):** `inset 0 1.5px 1.5px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` simulating direct overhead light bouncing off a beveled crystal rim.
3. **Refractive Underside (Bottom Inset):** `inset 0 -1.5px 2px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)` grounding the physical thickness of the glass plate.
4. **Specular Edge Stroke:** `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` on light surfaces; `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.22)` on deep midnight containers.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Floor):** The warm `#F6F1E9` background canvas and inset grouped form sections.
- **Level 1 (Liquid Glass Panel):** Standard expense cards, transaction items, and budget trackers.
  `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.8), inset 0 -1px 2px 0 rgba(10,46,68,0.06), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(10, 46, 68, 0.08);`
- **Level 2 (Tactile Glass Pill / Action):** Raised interactive controls, floating segment switchers, and elevated pill buttons.
  `box-shadow: inset 0 2px 2px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.95), inset 0 -2px 3px 0 rgba(10,46,68,0.1), 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 46, 68, 0.14);`
- **Level 3 (Modal / Floating Dock):** Bottom navigation dock and manual expense capture modal.
  `box-shadow: inset 0 2px 3px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.9), 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(10, 46, 68, 0.22);`

## Shapes

The shape system is defined by smooth pill contours and generous curvature:

- **Interactive Controls (Pills):** Full pill curvature (`roundedness: 3` / `9999px`) applied to all action buttons, input capsules, status chips, segmented switchers, and icon housings. The pill curvature mirrors liquid surface tension, intensifying glass edge refractions.
- **Surface Cards & Budget Containers:** Rounded corners (`1.5rem` to `2rem` / `24px` to `32px`) preserving continuous curvature (squircle geometry) that eliminates harsh structural vertices.
- **Nested Inner Modules:** Inset transaction rows and sub-metrics adopt `1rem` (`16px`) corners, nested concentrically within the parent card curvature.

## Components

### Buttons & Tactile Capsules
- **Liquid Pill Primary Button:** Raised pill (`rounded-full`) crafted with translucent glass fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`, topped with an upper-rim highlight (`inset 0 2px 2px 0 rgba(255,255,255,1)`), a deep sapphire label (`#0A2E44`), and a tactile elevation shadow. Hover increases inner luminance to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`. Active/press compresses the vertical displacement (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Turquoise Glass Accent Button:** Tinted liquid turquoise fill (`rgba(153, 225, 217, 0.45)`), `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, `1px` translucent white border, with bold `#0A2E44` type. Used for "Add Transaction" and primary budget confirmations.
- **Dark Glass Container Button:** Pill container with midnight fill `rgba(10, 46, 68, 0.85)`, crisp top specular sheen, and Pure White text.

### Segmented Switchers & Tabs
- Contained within an elongated liquid pill tray (`rgba(16, 35, 45, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.6)`).
- Active segment slides as a raised glass pill with convex top sheen, white backdrop blur, and crisp drop shadow, creating a visible 3D floating puck.

### Input Fields & Manual Transaction Form
- Text, amount, and date inputs are enclosed within frosted glass capsules (`48px` height, `rounded-full`).
- Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- Inset shadow provides a subtle concave well effect (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(10, 46, 68, 0.08)`).
- Currency indicator `₹` is fixed in JetBrains Mono (`#667981`), while input amounts display in bold `#10232D`.
- Focus state activates an external Turquoise ambient glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(153, 225, 217, 0.45)`).

### Metric & Budget Progress Cards
- High-level metric panels feature a multi-layer frosted white backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`) over the ivory base.
- Category budget bars feature an inset glass groove with a glowing filled progress pill in Income Green (`#15846C`), Signature Turquoise (`#99E1D9`), or Expense Coral (`#D96969`).
- Hero Balance Card uses a Midnight Sapphire glass container (`#0A2E44` at 92% opacity) with white rim refraction, displaying the main Rupee balance in large monospaced type with turquoise secondary accents.

### Badges & Trend Indicators
- Compact liquid pills (`height: 24px`, padding: `2px 10px`, `rounded-full`).
- Positive Cash Flow: Tinted green glass (`rgba(21, 132, 108, 0.12)`), Income Green text, with subtle top-edge sheen.
- Over-Budget / Expense Alert: Tinted coral glass (`rgba(217, 105, 105, 0.12)`), Expense Coral text.

### Lists & Transaction Ledgers
- Individual ledger items sit within stacked glass tiles separated by `8px` gaps.
- Merchant category avatars use circular liquid lenses (`40px x 40px`, `rounded-full`) with centered category icons.
- Rupee amounts are right-aligned in JetBrains Mono: `- ₹450.00` in Expense Coral or `+ ₹12,000.00` in Income Green.
